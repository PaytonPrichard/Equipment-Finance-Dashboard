Deal screening and covenant monitoring for asset-based lenders.

Asset classes  Equipment · AR · Inventory
Output  Committee-ready memo
Time to screen  Under two minutes

A deal arrives as a pile of documents that disagree with each other. Tranche reads them, tells you where they disagree, scores the deal against your credit policy, and produces the memo.

How it works

01

Upload the file

Credit application, financials, equipment quote, broker email. Up to four at once. Each is classified and read in parallel.

02

Review the merge

Fields populate from the document best placed to know. Disagreements are surfaced with both sources, never silently resolved.

03

Score and export

Pass, flag or fail against your thresholds, with a memo that opens on the request and closes by naming its sources.

The part that matters

A broker email says EBITDA is just under $7.9M. The reviewed financials say $7.4M. Most extraction tools pick one. Tranche picks the financials, because financial statements outrank a broker summary on financial data, and then tells you it did:

3 fields where the documents disagree
EBITDA: 7,400,000 from 02_financial-statements.pdf. 04_broker-email.txt says 7,900,000. Use that
Revenue: 38,400,000 from 02_financial-statements.pdf. 04_broker-email.txt says 38,000,000. Use that
Company: Granite Ridge Materials LLC from 01_credit-application.pdf. 04_broker-email.txt says Granite Ridge Materials. Use that

Precedence is set per kind of fact, not once globally. The dealer quote wins on equipment. The application wins on borrower identity. The statements win on money. Every merged field records which document supplied it, and that list is printed in the memo.

What it does not do

  • It does not write the memo's prose. One model reads documents. It never scores anything. Every sentence in the memo is a figure you confirmed or a template chosen by a numeric threshold.
  • It does not decide. Output is preliminary screening, not a credit decision, and says so on the page.
  • It does not predict default. Scores are screening-level risk assessments, not validated probabilities.
  • It does not appraise. Equipment and inventory values are estimates, not appraisals.
  • It does not replace your servicing platform. It sits in front of it.
