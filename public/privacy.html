Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Tranche is a deal screening tool for commercial credit teams. This page explains what we store, why, and what we do not do with it. It is written to be read, not to be comprehensive legal cover.

What we store

  • Account information. Your email address, name, organization, and role.
  • Deal data. The borrower and facility details you enter or upload, the scores computed from them, your notes, and your pipeline stages.
  • Documents. Files you upload to a deal are stored so they stay attached to it. You can delete them from the deal at any time.
  • Activity records. Changes to deals, stages, and facilities are recorded so your team has an audit trail.

Where it lives

Data is held in a managed Postgres database and object storage operated by Supabase, and the application is served by Vercel. Both encrypt data in transit and at rest at the infrastructure level. Each organization's rows are isolated from every other organization's by database row-level security policies, not only by application code.

Document processing

When you upload a deal document, its contents are sent to Anthropic's API to extract field values, which are returned to you for review before anything is scored or saved. Anthropic does not train models on data submitted through its API. If you would rather not send a document to a third party, enter the figures manually instead; every field is editable by hand.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.
  • We do not use your deal data to train models.
  • We do not share one organization's deals with another.
  • We do not run third-party analytics or advertising trackers on the application.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays until you delete it or ask us to. Deleting a deal removes the deal record; deleting an attachment removes the file. To have an organization's data removed entirely, email us and we will do it and confirm when it is done.

Your choices

You can export your pipeline and any screening to CSV or PDF at any time from inside the app. You can request a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by email.
