What the service does

Tranche scores commercial credit opportunities against configurable thresholds and produces a committee-ready memo. Scores are computed from the inputs you confirm. Where documents are uploaded, extracted values are presented for your review and are not scored until you accept them.

What it does not do

  • It does not predict default. Scores are screening-level risk assessments, not validated probabilities of default.
