- TIMING: last 10-K before the 2025 Herc acquisition. Screen is as of February 2025.

**Hertz Global Holdings, Inc.**

- ESTIMATE, maintenanceCapex: Non-vehicle depreciation ($95M) used as maintenance capex proxy. Vehicle depreciation is already deducted in corporate EBITDA. Actual non-vehicle capex was $97M.
- JUDGMENT, EBITDA definition: GAAP build used (pre-tax -830 + non-vehicle interest 469 + non-vehicle D&A 117 = -$244M). Company-reported Adjusted EBITDA in the segment note (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1657853/000165785326000008/R114.htm) is -$48M for the two segments combined and -$291M for Corporate. It adds back items management treats as one-off. All three readings are negative, so the direction of the verdict does not depend on the choice.
- JUDGMENT, interest: statement of operations shows non-vehicle interest of $469M. The segment note shows "non-vehicle debt interest, net" of $498M. Different measures. $469M used.
- SYNTHETIC STRUCTURE: Hertz does not fund its fleet with single-lender equipment finance. The deal terms are hypothetical and test the corporate credit read, not a realistic structure.
- RATING TIMING: issuer rating at the 10-K filing date was B- or B (date of the B to B- cut unconfirmed). Both map to Weak. The CCC+ downgrade on 2026-08-12 is after the screen date and not used.
- JUDGMENT, cash-flow inputs: corporate-level working capital and rent are approximations. Accrued liabilities may include vehicle-related items, and variable airport concession rent ($274M) is left out of rent. EBITDA is negative, so the verdict does not depend on these.
- STRESS: EBITDA is negative, so the EBITDA-decline scenarios shrink the loss and read better than the base case (AUDIT P2-13). Ignore the stress table for this case.

**Titan Machinery Inc.**

- ESTIMATE, maintenanceCapex: Depreciation (D&A $37.6M less intangible amortization $1.8M) used as maintenance capex proxy.
- JUDGMENT, floorplan: treated as inventory financing. Floorplan interest is deducted in EBITDA and the $553.8M floorplan payable is left out of debt. This follows auto-dealer credit convention, not verified against Titan's own credit agreement. Including floorplan instead: EBITDA $30.9M, debt $783.0M.
- ESTIMATE, debt service: uses non-floorplan interest expense ($19.0M), not cash paid. Cash interest is reported only in total.
- RATING: treated as Not Rated (+100 bps) because S&P shows no rating. Moody's was not checked. If Titan is rated B or below somewhere, the input becomes Weak (+200 bps).
- JUDGMENT, working capital: includes both floorplan paydowns ($226.7M), consistent with treating floorplan as inventory financing. Leaving out the non-manufacturer floorplan line (it sits in financing activities) would show a $145.2M cash release that actually went to floorplan lenders. Either way the release is one-off, from shrinking inventory, and not repeatable.
- NOTE, cash taxes: a net refund of $1.8M, which adds to cash flow this year. Not a recurring source.
