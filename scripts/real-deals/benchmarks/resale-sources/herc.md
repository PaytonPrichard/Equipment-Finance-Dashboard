## Conflicts and restatements

1. **Q2 2020**: Q2 2020 deck bullet says "~38% proceeds as a % of OEC". Q3 2020 and Q4 2020 roll-forward tables say 40.7%. Not a rounding gap. Both recorded.
2. **Q1 2020**: Q1 2020 deck bullet says "~36%". Later roll-forward tables say 37.1%. Both recorded.
3. **FY2021 disposals**: (286) in decks from Q4 2021 through Q1 2023, (281) in decks from Q4 2025 onward. The 2021 quarterly figures (111, 71, 44, 60) sum to 286 and come only from the older decks. The newer decks do not restate the quarters, so which quarters moved is unknown.
4. **FY2020 disposals**: (551) in older decks, (552) in Q4 2024 and later decks. FY2020 "Foreign Currency / Other" also changed from (31) to (57) and later to (11) once Acquisitions (28) and Divestiture (46) were broken out (D25Q3 s27).
5. Other quarters reported in more than one deck matched exactly across every deck checked.
6. All other headline bullets agree with the table within rounding.

## Gaps

- **Average age of disposals** is missing for Q4 2024, Q3 2024 (bullets omit it), Q2 to Q4 2023 (decks not on EDGAR), Q1 2019, Q2 2019 and all of 2017 to 2018 (not disclosed in those decks). The coverage that exists runs Q4 2020 to Q1 2023, Q1 to Q2 2024 and Q1 2025 to Q2 2026, plus Q3 and Q4 2019 and Q1 to Q3 2020.
- **Average fleet age** is missing for Q2 to Q4 2023 and Q1 2017.
- **Quarterly proceeds in dollars** are not disclosed. Only YTD cash proceeds exist (table above).
- **Definition of "Proceeds as a percent of OEC"**: no glossary definition found in any deck or the 10-K. The only qualifier is the roll-forward footnote "1. Original equipment cost based on ARA guidelines."
- Q2 2026 deck slide numbering was checked against the slide images. Other decks' slide numbers come from page breaks in the filed HTML. In the Q1 2023 and earlier decks the printed footer number sits at the end of the slide text, which can mislead. The page-break count is what I used.

## Verbatim definitions

**OEC** (deck glossary, "Glossary of Terms Commonly Use[d] in the Industry", D26Q2 s20; same wording in D25Q4 s20, D20Q1 s22, D19Q2 s25):
> "OEC: Original Equipment Cost which is an operating measure based on the guidelines of the American Rental Association (ARA), which is calculated as the cost of the asset at the time it was first purchased plus additional capitalized refurbishment costs (with the basis of refurbished assets reset at the refurbishment date)."

**Fleet Age** (same glossary, D26Q2 s20):
> "Fleet Age: The OEC weighted age of the entire fleet, based on ARA guidelines."

Older decks (D17Q3 s31, D17Q4 s29, D18Q1 s29) read "Fleet Age: The OEC weighted age of the entire fleet." without the ARA clause.

**Roll-forward footnote** (every roll-forward slide, e.g. D26Q2 s29):
> "1. Original equipment cost based on ARA guidelines."

D22Q4 s32 and D23Q1 s30 add: "(2) Other includes acquisitions and divestitures of businesses".

**Net Fleet Capital Expenditures** (D26Q2 s20):
> "Net Fleet Capital Expenditures: Capital expenditures of rental equipment minus the proceeds from disposal of rental equipment."

**10-K fleet age and OEC** (K25, Item 1 Business):
> "As of December 31, 2025, the average age of our equipment rental fleet was 45 months."
> "As of December 31, 2025, our rental fleet consisted of equipment with a total original equipment cost, based on the guidelines of the American Rental Association, of $9.5 billion."

## Verbatim: used-equipment sales channels

K25, Item 1 Business, "Sales of Used Rental Equipment":
> "We routinely sell our used rental equipment to manage repair and maintenance costs, as well as the composition, age and size of our fleet. We dispose of our used equipment through a variety of channels, including retail sales to customers and other third parties, sales to wholesalers, brokered sales and auctions."

K25, Item 7 MD&A (2025 vs 2024):
> "Sales of rental equipment increased $198 million, or 64%, during 2025 when compared with 2024 as we increased the volume of sales to improve the equipment mix and utilization focusing on acquisition fleet. The margin on sales of rental equipment was 18% in 2025 compared to 28% in 2024. The decrease in margin sale of rental equipment in 2025 resulted from the fair value markup of the acquisition fleet sold, a larger volume of sales through the lower margin auction channel and continued normalization of used equipment pricing in the market."

D25Q1 s9:
> "Q1 25 disposals generated proceeds of ~45% of OEC ◦ Strong execution of sales channel-management strategy"

D25Q1 s16 and D24Q4 s19 (outlook):
> "Used equipment market normalizing; continue the shift to higher-margin retail/wholesale sales channels"

D24Q3 s6:
> "Favorable shift of fleet dispositions to retail/wholesale channel offsets normalizing auction proceeds YoY"

D24Q3 s16:
> "Fewer fleet dispositions YoY but higher average proceeds from favorable sales channel mix"

No quantified retail vs auction split was found in any deck or the 10-K.
