# Iowa State University Extension, Ag Decision Maker File A3-29 "Estimating Farm Machinery Costs" (PM 710, Revised March 2026), William Edwards
- URL: https://www.extension.iastate.edu/agdm/crops/pdf/a3-29.pdf (HTML landing: https://www.extension.iastate.edu/agdm/crops/html/a3-29.html)
- Tables 1a and 1b, "Remaining salvage value as percent of new list price." Source line: "American Society of Agricultural and Biological Engineers (ASABE)."

> "The factors were developed from published reports of used equipment auction values, and are estimates of the average "as-is" value of a class of machines in average mechanical condition at the farm."

Values below were read from a PDF text extraction with shifted row labels. Alignment was checked against the document's two worked examples ("the salvage value after 12 years is estimated as 28% of the new list price" for a 300-hp tractor at 400 hrs/yr, and a plow at 14 years = 30%). Both matched.
- 150+ hp tractor, 400 hrs/yr: age 3 = 54%, age 5 = 45%, age 7 = 39%, age 10 = 32%
- 80-149 hp tractor, 400 hrs/yr: age 5 = 49%, age 7 = 44%, age 10 = 37%
- Combine/forage harvester, 300 hrs/yr: age 3 = 50%, age 5 = 39%, age 7 = 31%, age 10 = 23%
- Planter/drill/sprayer: age 5 = 50%, age 10 = 40%
- "Other" machines: age 5 = 48%, age 10 = 35%

Basis: estimated MARKET (auction) value, % of NEW LIST PRICE (the worked example uses list $400,000 vs purchase $350,000, so % of actual cost would be higher). Not a booked lease residual. Not guaranteed.
