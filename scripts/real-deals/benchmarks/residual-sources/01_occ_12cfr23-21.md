# OCC 12 CFR 23.21 (national bank leases, estimated residual value)
- URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/12/23.21 (eCFR redirected to a bot check, so the LII copy was used)
- Issued 61 FR 66560 (Dec. 18, 1996), amended 66 FR 34792 (July 2, 2001). Opened 2026-10-12.
- Note: the fetch tool returned verbatim fragments capped at 125 characters, not the full section text.

§23.21(a), estimate of residual value for a full-payout lease:
> "Must be reasonable in light of the nature of the leased property and all circumstances relevant to the transaction"
> "Any unguaranteed amount must not exceed 25 percent of the original cost of the property to the bank"
> "or the percentage for a particular type of property specified in published OCC guidance"

§23.21(b), guaranteed residual:
> "may exceed 25 percent of the original cost of the property if the bank determines"
> "the guarantor has the resources to meet the guarantee and the guarantor is not an affiliate of the bank"

Basis: regulatory ceiling on the unguaranteed residual a national bank may rely on in a full-payout lease. % of original cost. Not a market benchmark. Binds banks only.
